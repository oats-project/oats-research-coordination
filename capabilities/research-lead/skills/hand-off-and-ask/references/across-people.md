# Across people
Each person owns areas: the ownership record in your landing rules says who. Read it
before you plan. Your person's yes covers your person's choices only, and you can't spawn,
retire or direct another person's agents: you ask.
- **One effort, one lead:** the expert whose specialisation fits the effort best,
  whichever people's areas it crosses. A finding written into another person's area
  (their knowledge, their reports) is written by that person's area expert.
- **A mechanical correction in their area** (a dead link, a date format, a broken
  citation) needs nobody's approval. Say so on the record you corrected.
- **A change to what their area holds** (a claim in their reports or their knowledge that
  your report changes, or a report that decides something in their area): write to that
  person directly, in a new thread on the effort: who you are, what the effort is, its
  link, and that you lead it. Then one card for the whole effort, before the work in
  their area starts. Their answer comes in that thread; record it. No card goes through a
  dispatcher, and no person relays another's decision.
- **Their experts:** bring in the one on the effort by a hand-off; where none is, ask
  their dispatcher to start the one you need, with your message by its id as the request
  it reads; where they have no dispatcher, ask them. Never address their researchers or
  verifiers.
- **Landing:** each area's landing rules apply. A report lands on the acceptance of the
  person who accepts it; a finding for another person's area goes into their knowledge
  by their area's expert. Where an area asks for its maintainer's green light, it is the
  maintainer of the person who owns that area.
- **Never change what another person's area holds** without their decision on the effort.
- **Disputes.** Text (two efforts revise the same knowledge entry): the first one landed
  wins, and the second revises on top of it. Meaning, between agents: post your evidence
  once on the effort's record and send it to the generalist expert, which settles it;
  where you are the generalist and a party, start a fresh, independent instance of it for
  that, with the evidence and the options as its task: it settles the dispute and
  retires. Between people: put the evidence and the options to both, once, as one card.
  No agent decides between its person and another.
